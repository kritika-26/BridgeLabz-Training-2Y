PROBLEM 3: CITY ROAD NETWORK

Representation:
Weighted Directed Graph

Edges:
A→B(5), B↔C(3), A↔D(7), D→E(2), C→E(4)

1. Reachable from A:
Using BFS:
A → B, D → C → E

2. Fewest Turns (BFS):
Path:
A → D → E

3. DFS Limitation:
DFS may go A→B→C→E (longer path)
So not shortest

Time Complexity: O(V + E)
